Corporate Partnerships Program

Why partner with us.

Since 1890, the Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory has pushed the frontiers of astronomy, from early solar research to helping produce the first image of a black hole.

Today the Observatory is part of one of the world's largest astrophysics research communities, bringing together scientists and engineers to study the universe and build the tools needed to understand it.

The Corporate Partnership Program connects organizations to that work, with direct access to the people, facilities, and systems behind it.

The questions we ask.

We ask big questions about the universe, craft the strategies and tools to answer them, and share the results with the world:

  • How do stars and planets form?

  • How did the universe begin?

  • What do black holes look like?

  • Does life exist beyond Earth?

What you make possible.

Corporate partners directly support the infrastructure and minds required to make these discoveries. Your gifts support:

  • The design and construction of telescopes, detectors, and scientific instruments.
  • The operation of observatories and research facilities in Cambridge, Arizona, Hawaii, and international sites.
  • The research of graduate students, postdoctoral researchers, and early-career scientists.
  • National science education programs for schools and educators.

The partnership experience.

Partnerships offer direct, tiered access to the Observatory's people, facilities, and programs. All partnerships are built upon three core pillars:

  • Access

    Briefings with scientists and research leaders, program invitations, and facility visits.

  • Engagement

    Custom virtual or in-person discussions tied to your organization's interests, and opportunities to connect your technical teams with our researchers.

  • Visibility

    Recognition across Smithsonian platforms and acknowledgment in publications and events.

Levels of support.

Engagements are based on availability and aligned with scientific priorities. Six tiers offer direct access to the Observatory's people, facilities, and programs.

Premium engagement opportunities.

The Corporate Leadership Council

Available exclusively to Vanguard and Luminary partners.

This group includes senior representatives from partner organizations. Participants receive access to highly private briefings, exclusive Observatory networks, and select opportunities for deeper, strategic engagement with our leadership.

Naming Opportunities

For organizations seeking permanent, long-term visibility, the Observatory offers naming opportunities that connect a partner's name directly to the places and programs where research is carried out. Opportunities include:

  • Research and engineering spaces
  • Observatories, facilities, and physical sites
  • Data and scientific programs
  • Fellowships and endowed positions

Partner with us.

Corporate support makes it possible to build new instruments, operate observatories, train the next generation of scientists, and expand access to science education. Support discovery that is already underway.
